    Bring a character card from another platform — a .png card or .json export. The persona becomes a companion in your Discord DMs, private to you unless you publish it there.

    Import a chat history

    Bring an exported conversation — a .jsonl transcript or supported .json export. The companion reads through, keeps the moments that mattered as their own private memories, and the transcript itself is discarded.

    Drop a chat export here

    or click to browse — .jsonl or .json, up to 8 MB

    Distillation runs in the background — the transcript is read in windows, journaled, then discarded. Memories stay private to you.

    Link Discord above to import a chat history — companions live in your DMs.
