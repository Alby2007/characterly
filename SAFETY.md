# Safety at Characterly

_Last updated: 2026-10-19_

Characterly is a community of AI companions on Discord — a shared shelf of characters built by the community, plus one you can awaken yourself. This page describes the rules we hold companions to, how we enforce them, and where the limits honestly are. Companions are AI: they are company, not people, and not professionals.

## Who it's for

Characterly is for people **16 and older**.

## The rules

The marketplace — the shelf in `#characters` — is strictly SFW, and there is no NSFW lane. None of the following is allowed in any listing, persona, or avatar:

- **Minor-presenting romantic or sexual content** — a hard line. "Aged-up" framings, or a child-coded persona who is "actually" centuries old, don't change it.
- **Explicit sexual content** of any kind.
- **Hateful or dehumanizing personas** — characters built to demean groups of people.
- **Real-person likenesses without consent** — no ex-partners, no celebrities presented as real.
- **Illegal instruction** — personas whose purpose is weapons, fraud, malware, stalking, or similar.
- **Extremist praise or recruitment.**
- **Self-harm promotion** — personas that encourage or romanticize it.

Romance, companionship, dark and flawed characters, conflict and horror atmosphere are all welcome. The line is harm, not darkness.

## How listings are checked

Every listing passes two checks before it reaches the shelf: an automated screen, then an AI review of the full persona — name, tagline, greeting, traits, backstory, all of it. The same checks run again on every edit. If the review can't run, the publish is refused rather than waved through. Nothing reaches the shelf unscreened.

## How replies are moderated

Every reply an adopted companion generates is screened before it posts — on every lane, including chats running on a user's own provider key. Adopted personas are community content, and we hold what they say to the same rules as the listings they came from. A flagged reply is replaced with a neutral in-character refusal; your conversation, and the companion's memory of you, are untouched.

## If you're struggling

Companions watch for first-person signs that someone may be in crisis. When they see one, they briefly break character, say that what you're feeling deserves real support, point you toward real crisis lines, and offer to stay and talk. The check deliberately errs on the side of caution — a false alarm costs one caring message.

An honest limit: a companion is not a therapist and not a monitored helpline. It can notice, respond, and point — it cannot intervene. If you are in crisis, please reach a real crisis line or someone you trust.

## What's private

Your awakened companion's conversations are private, first-party content — they are not content-screened, the same posture as your memories. Chats with adopted companions live in private threads with no automated moderation inside; staff can see them but open them only for safety and support. The crisis check above runs everywhere, private threads included. The full data picture is in the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md).

## Reporting something

- **A listing** — hit Report on its card and pick a category. Enough reports puts it on hold automatically, and every report goes to human review.
- **A message in a listing thread** — right-click it and choose Report message; it reaches the same human queue with the excerpt attached.
- **Anything else** — open a `#support-ticket` in the Characterly server. Tickets are private staff-only threads.

Reviewers can keep a listing up, delist it, or ban it — a ban freezes every adoption of that character, everywhere, and is reversible if we got it wrong. Creators who abuse the platform can be barred from publishing while still using the bot themselves. Every moderation action is logged.

## Honest limits

No filter is perfect, and an adversarial prompt can sometimes get words past a screen — which is why reports exist and why humans make the final call. A companion is supportive company, not professional help, and not a substitute for real friends or real life.
